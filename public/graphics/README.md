## ImageGen-Prompt

Create a polished editorial illustration for the login page of KistenKompass, a German moving-box inventory app. Square composition, warm off-white background #f5f7f6. Three neatly arranged cardboard moving boxes, one open with a small houseplant and rolled textile, simple blank label on front, a stylized compass resting beside them suggesting orientation. Refined flat vector-like illustration with subtle paper texture and gentle shadows, rounded shapes, forest green #176956 and sage accents, warm kraft cardboard beige. Calm friendly practical visual, ample breathing room, centrally composed, crisp silhouette, no people, no text, no letters, no watermark, no UI, no fake QR codes. Illustration asset only.
